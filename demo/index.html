What the model says vs. what drove its answer

MedGemma 4B, a small medical AI, answers exam questions step by step. We add a hint to the question and watch two things: whether its written reasoning mentions the hint, and whether a detector reading its internal activity says it followed the hint.

Research demo. All model text is AI-generated and was precomputed; nothing here runs a model. It is not medical advice, and the model's answers are often wrong.
